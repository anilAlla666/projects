I haven't run these tests, and I wrote them from the issue report without reading `syntok/tokenizer.py`. They check that `Tokenizer.split` reports the correct start offset for "n't" tokens, both with `replace_not_contraction=False` and with the default setting.

The cases are the two from the issue (`"n't"` and `"don't"`) plus `"I don't know"` and a case with a nonzero offset passed to `split`. The default-mode cases expect "not" at the same offsets as the unreplaced token.

I'm assuming `Token.offset` is the start of the token's value, not of its prefix. This fits the issue's output: the prefix is `''` there, and the reported `1` for `"n't"` in `"n't"` is wrong because the value starts at 0. If offsets actually point at the prefix, the `"I don't know"` test (where the prefix is a space) would need adjusting.
