They compare weight values and weighted results rather than index labels, so they leave open how the weights' index is handled after subsetting. I did not test `d2.mean()` on the whole frame, since the issue doesn't say what it should return. The tests use `d2.x.mean()` and `d2.y.sum()` instead.
