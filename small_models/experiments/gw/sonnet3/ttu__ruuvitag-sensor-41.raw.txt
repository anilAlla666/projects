The trimmed payload is the existing `FF:2C:6A:1E:59:3D` sample with its `00000000` padding removed. I also adjusted the two length bytes to `15` and `11` so the packet stays internally consistent.

`test_trimmed_format_3_is_decoded` checks that `get_data_for_sensors` returns the same decoded values for the trimmed and padded packets, plus temperature 26.3 and humidity 20.5. `test_find_tags_with_trimmed_format_3` checks that `find_ruuvitags` recognises the trimmed tag.

I derived the expected temperature and humidity by hand from the sample bytes (humidity `0x29` ÷ 2, temperature `0x1A.0x1E`). I did not check them against the decoder, so those two assertions are the most likely to need adjusting if my reading of Format 3 is off.
