## Single biggest doc-vs-code gap

**`t4_6_4_report.md` declares "Phase 4 closes" — but Phase 4 has two unstarted sub-phases.**

`t4_6_4_report.md:11` and `:75-77` state: *"Phase 4 closure gate: all five indicators PASS … All five indicators PASS → **Phase 4 closes.**"*

This is contradicted by the project's own binding spec. `PHASE_4_ARCHITECTURE.md:134-135` defines **P4.7** (Fusion + agentic + compliance — FUSION, FLOW_*, SENSE, SHIELD, LOOP, PIPELINE, FAIRNESS, …) and **P4.8** (Integration + G3 density push + **G4 24-hour soak** + evidence tarball) as required Phase 4 sub-phases, and `:151` states "Final P4.8 ship gate is the four G1–G4 criteria … measured simultaneously on real silicon under WL05 multi-tenant load."

Code reality: **no P4.7 or P4.8 work product exists** — no fusion runtime files in `cipher_rt_phase4/`, no soak run log, no density CSV, no P4.7/P4.8 report. The T4.6.4 report's "Phase 4 closes" claim accounts only for the KV-dedup cluster (P4.6) and silently treats the KV-dedup gate as the phase gate. T4.6.4 itself further hedges in the same document (`:78-79`) that "T4.6.5 … is the first **Phase 5** deliverable" — i.e. the report simultaneously closes Phase 4 and pushes its own next step into Phase 5, skipping P4.7 and P4.8 entirely. The honest status is that **Phase 4 is IN-FLIGHT**: 4.3 and 4.5 shipped (with regime caveats), 4.6 partially shipped (T4.6.1–4 done, T4.6.5–6 not), 4.4 partial, and **4.7 + 4.8 not started**.
